ABOUT PETROL PRICES

Petrol Price in Pakistan

Petrol prices in Pakistan can change based on government pricing decisions, international oil prices, exchange rates, taxes and other applicable costs.

How is the petrol price determined?

The retail petrol price is influenced by international petroleum prices, the Pakistani rupee exchange rate, government levies and other components included in the official pricing formula.

When can petrol prices change?

Petrol prices may be revised when the government announces a new pricing decision. Daily Rate PK updates the displayed rate when verified rate data is available.

How can I calculate my petrol cost?

You can estimate your fuel expense by entering your journey distance and vehicle mileage in the Fuel Cost Calculator.
