About Daily Rate PK

Pakistan's daily rates, prices and useful calculators in one place.

What is Daily Rate PK?

Daily Rate PK is a price and rate information website focused on Pakistan. We aim to make commonly searched daily rates easy to find in one simple and mobile-friendly place.

What We Cover

Our website covers fuel prices, gold and silver rates, currency exchange rates, LPG prices, price history and useful calculators.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide clear, useful and regularly updated information while making it easy for visitors to compare rates and calculate prices.
