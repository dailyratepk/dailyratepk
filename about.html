About Daily Rate PK

Daily Rate PK is an independent information website designed to make commonly searched prices and rates in Pakistan easier to find, understand and compare on both mobile phones and desktop devices.

What is Daily Rate PK?

Daily Rate PK brings important Pakistan price information together in one convenient place. Visitors can check fuel prices, precious metal rates, currency information and LPG prices without having to search through multiple pages. Our goal is to present this information in a clear and easy-to-use format.

What We Cover

We cover petrol and diesel prices, gold and silver rates, USD to PKR and other currency information, LPG prices, selected price history and practical online calculators. Each section is organized around information that people commonly look for when checking daily prices in Pakistan.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide useful information in a simple format and keep rate information as current as possible. We also provide calculators that can help visitors estimate fuel costs, convert currencies, calculate gold values and perform percentage calculations.
